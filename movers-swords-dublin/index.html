Movers in Swords: House, Apartment & Long Distance Removals

Swords is the biggest town in Fingal and one of the fastest growing areas in north county Dublin, with new estates going up around Applewood and Forrest Little alongside the older village core near Swords Castle. Its position next to Dublin Airport also means we do more airport-adjacent and long-distance moves here than almost anywhere else. Here's what a move in Swords typically involves.

Moving House in Swords: What to Expect

Swords has grown outward fast, from the traditional village around Main Street and Swords Castle, to large modern estates in Applewood, Forrest Little, Rivervalley and Lissenhall. Newer estates tend to have easier van access and driveways, while village-area homes can have tighter on-street parking, both matter for planning a move, which is exactly why Moving Service Dublin scopes the job properly before the day. The Swords Area We Cover

We cover Swords village, Applewood, Forrest Little, Rivervalley, Lissenhall, Boroimhe, Seatown and out towards the airport and Malahide Road. Swords Pavilions Shopping Centre is the main retail hub, with Fingal County Council's headquarters based in the town and Swords Castle marking the older village centre.

Local Moving Tips for Swords

  • Airport-bound traffic can back up the M1 and R132 at peak times, especially early morning, plan your slot around this if you're moving near the airport.
  • Village-area streets near Main Street can be tighter for larger trucks, a van-based move usually suits these better.
  • If you're moving abroad or interstate via the airport, book well ahead, long distance jobs need more lead time to plan logistics properly.
  • Newer estates like Forrest Little and Lissenhall generally have easier access and driveway parking for loading.
