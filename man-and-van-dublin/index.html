A man and van Dublin service is the simplest way to move when a full removal crew would be overkill. At Moving Service Dublin you get one transit van and one driver who loads, drives and unloads, so you are only paying for the help you need.

It suits a huge range of jobs, from a bedsit move in Phibsborough to collecting a dining table in Tallaght. Have a look at our services or our same day man and van page if you need someone quickly.

What Fits in a Transit Van?

People are often surprised by how much a transit van holds. As a rough guide, the contents of a studio or one bed flat usually fit in one trip, including a double bed, a sofa, a wardrobe, a small table and chairs and around 20 to 30 boxes. A two bed apartment may fit in one load if it is lightly furnished, or need a second run if there is a lot of furniture.

The easiest way to know for sure is to send us a list or a few photos. We will tell you honestly whether it is one trip or two and price it that way, so there are no surprises on the day.

Booking Tips

Give us the full address at both ends, including the Eircode, so we can plan the route and check parking. Tell us the floor number and whether there is a lift. If the street has permit parking, as many do around Rathmines, Ranelagh and Stoneybatter, ask a neighbour if we can use their space for an hour or arrange a visitor permit.

Man and Van Dublin, Done With Care

A man and van job should not mean things thrown in the back loose. We wrap furniture in thick moving blankets, pad anything with glass or a polished surface and strap the load securely so it does not shift on the road. Mattresses are covered to keep them clean, and we take care with walls, banisters and door frames on the way in and out. We are fully insured for every job, big or small.

You also get a fixed price agreed before the day. That means no clock ticking while we wait at traffic lights on the South Circular Road, and no extra added on at the end. If the job details are clear up front, the price stays the same.

Can I Come Along in the Van?

For most jobs, it is best to travel separately in your own car or on public transport so you can arrive at the new address with the keys ready. Let us know your plans when you book and we will arrange timings so you are there when we pull up.
