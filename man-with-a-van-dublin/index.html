Understanding Man with a Van Services

"Man with a van" covers a wide range of jobs that don't need a full removals crew: a single sofa, a few boxes between flats, a charity shop drop off, or a small flat share move. It's built to be flexible and priced fairly for smaller loads, rather than charging you for a service bigger than you need. If you're moving a full house, our House Removals Dublin service is usually a better fit, but for anything from one item to a van load, man with a van is the quicker, more affordable option.

Man with a Van in Dublin: What to Expect

Vehicle Sizes and What Fits

Our vans handle anything from a single wardrobe to several rooms' worth of furniture and boxes. If you're not sure what will fit, describe what you're moving when you message us and we'll size the job correctly rather than sending a van that's too small or charging you for space you don't need.

Getting Around Dublin's Traffic and Parking

Dublin's one way systems, bus lanes, and permit parking catch out drivers who don't know the city. We plan routes and drop off points in advance, particularly for city centre addresses, so we're not wasting your time circling for parking once we arrive.

Same Day and Short Notice Availability

Plans change. If you need a van today rather than next week, check our Same Day Man and Van Dublin page, or simply message us your location and we'll confirm the soonest slot we have free.

How to Choose a Man with a Van Service

Questions to Ask

  • Is the price fixed or based on time, and what happens if it runs over?
  • Are they insured for the items you're moving?
  • Do they know your pickup and drop off area, including parking restrictions?
  • Can they confirm a realistic arrival window, not just "sometime today"?

Tips for Booking a Man with a Van

  1. Have everything ready to load before the van arrives to keep the job quick.
  2. Measure large items against doorways and stairwells beforehand.
  3. Let us know about any parking restrictions at either address.
  4. Ask for a fixed price rather than an hourly estimate where possible.
  5. Keep valuables and essentials with you rather than in the van.
