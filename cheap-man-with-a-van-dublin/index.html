If you need a cheap man with a van Dublin service, chances are your job is not a full house move. It might be a bedsit, a few boxes and a bike, or a wardrobe you picked up on Marketplace. That is exactly the kind of work Moving Service Dublin is set up for.

We run a single transit van with a driver who also does the lifting, so there is no paying for extra people standing around. Have a look at our services or read more about our standard man with a van Dublin service if you want the full picture.

Jobs That Suit a Man and a Van

A transit van holds more than most people expect. Typical jobs we do include:

  • Studio and one bed flat moves around the city
  • Moving a student from digs to a new house for the college year
  • Collecting a sofa, bed or table from a shop or private seller
  • Taking boxes and furniture to a family member's home
  • Moving a few rooms when you are downsizing or splitting a household

If you are not sure it will all fit, send us photos or a list. We will tell you honestly whether it is one trip or two before you book.

How to Keep a Man with a Van Job Cheap

Time on the job is what you are really paying for, so anything that saves time saves you money. Have everything packed and ready before the van pulls up. Boxes should be closed and light enough to carry comfortably, and bags should be tied rather than left open.

Clear a path from the room to the front door. If there is a lift in your building, check it is working and book it with management if needed. In places like the IFSC or Grand Canal Dock, ask about loading bays or visitor parking in advance, as a long carry from the street adds minutes fast.

Collections From Shops and Private Sellers

A lot of our smaller jobs are collections. Someone finds a dresser on DoneDeal in Terenure or a second hand fridge in Finglas and just needs it brought home. Before you book, check the item will fit through your door and up your stairs, measure it if you can, and confirm the seller will be home at the pickup time. If it is a flat pack shop collection, have your order number ready so we are not left waiting at the counter.

Cheap Man with a Van Dublin, Without the Shortcuts

A low price only matters if the job is done well. Every item we carry is wrapped in thick moving blankets, and anything fragile is padded and strapped in place so it does not shift on the road. We are fully insured, which means your belongings are covered while they are in our care.

You also get a fixed price quote agreed in advance. No hourly clock ticking while we sit in traffic on the quays, and no extra charge added at the end because the stairs were steeper than expected. We ask the right questions up front so the quote is accurate.

Sharing the Lifting

As a one van, one mover service, we are happy to do all of the carrying on most jobs. For very heavy single items, such as a solid oak wardrobe or a large chest freezer, it can help to have a friend on hand at one end to give a lift, and that can keep the job simpler. Let us know about any heavy pieces when you ask for a quote and we will plan the safest way to move them.
