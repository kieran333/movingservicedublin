Movers Templeogue Homeowners Choose

Much of Templeogue was built as family housing in the middle of the last century, with semi detached and detached homes on roads like Cypress Grove, Fortfield Road, Orwell Park and around Templeville Road. Most have driveways, which makes loading easier than in many parts of Dublin, but they also tend to hold a lot: full attics, garages and garden sheds built up over years.

When we plan a Templeogue move we ask about those spaces up front. Garden furniture, tools, bikes and boxes from the attic all take up room in the van, and knowing about them means your fixed price quote is accurate and the day runs to time.

Downsizing and Family Moves

We help a lot of people downsizing from a family home in Templeogue to an apartment or smaller house nearby, and just as many young families moving in. Downsizing moves benefit from a little extra planning:

  • Decide early what furniture will fit in the new home, and measure larger pieces.
  • Label boxes by room so we can place them where they belong.
  • Let us know if some items are going to family members at a different address, as we can often fit a short extra drop into the day.
  • Keep essentials like medication, documents and phone chargers with you.

Moving Near Bushy Park and the Dodder

Some of the most sought after roads in Templeogue back onto Bushy Park, Cypress Grove or the River Dodder walkways. These roads are quiet, but they are often lined with parked cars and mature trees, and the space in front of a house can be tighter than it looks. We check the street on a map before the day and, where needed, ask you to keep a space free outside so the van is not double parked. If you have a grand piano, a heavy dresser or a large garden table, let us know so we can plan the carry through the house and down the drive.

Local Roads and Getting Around

Templeogue Road and the village area are busy through the morning and evening, and traffic at Templeogue Bridge over the Dodder can back up at peak times. We plan our arrival around that. For moves heading further, the M50 is close by at Firhouse and Ballycullen, which gives quick access to the rest of Dublin and to long distance routes anywhere in Ireland.

We run a single transit van with a driver and mover. Furniture is wrapped and protected, strapped in the van and placed in the right rooms at the other end. For larger homes we plan the load and number of trips ahead, or arrange a larger vehicle when needed. We are fully insured, and same day moves are possible when the van is free.

If you are moving from Templeogue into an apartment, check early whether the new building needs a lift booking, and whether a transit van can get into the car park. Underground car parks often have height limits, and knowing this in advance saves a lot of carrying.
