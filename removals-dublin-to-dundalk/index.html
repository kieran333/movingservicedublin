The route from Dublin to Dundalk

We take the M50 to the M1 and head north past Dublin Airport, Balbriggan and Drogheda, then on to Dundalk. It is motorway nearly the whole way and the drive is usually around an hour from north Dublin, a bit longer from the south side once you allow for the M50 and Port Tunnel area.

Moves to nearby spots like Blackrock in Louth, Carlingford, Ardee or Castlebellingham are reached on regional roads at the end of the motorway section.

The M1 is generally free flowing outside rush hour, though traffic around the airport junction and the M50 interchange can build in the morning. We time departures to avoid the worst of it, so your belongings are not sitting in a queue on the motorway.

  • You send both addresses and a list of furniture and boxes

Because the drive is short, we can often complete a two bed apartment or small house in a single day with time to spare. For larger homes we plan trips in advance or arrange a bigger vehicle where needed.

Local knowledge for Dundalk moves

Dundalk town centre around Clanbrassil Street, Park Street and the Market Square is busy during the day, with limited loading space. If you are moving into a terraced house or flat in the centre, an early start helps. Estates on the edges of town, such as those off the Inner Relief Road or out towards Haggardstown and Blackrock, are generally easier to access with plenty of room for the van.

Moving to the Cooley Peninsula or a rural home in the county? Some of the hill roads near Carlingford and Omeath are narrow and winding, so let us know in advance and we will plan the approach.

Many people moving to Dundalk are first time buyers leaving a rented flat in Dublin. These moves are often smaller and suit our single van perfectly. We can also bring a few pieces up for a family member or collect furniture you have bought in Dublin and deliver it to Louth.

Tips for your move north

  • Keep important documents and valuables in your own car
  • Book an early slot to beat M1 traffic
  • Label boxes clearly by room
  • Sort keys for the new property before the day
