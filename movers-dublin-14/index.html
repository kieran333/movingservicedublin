We wrap every piece, take apart what needs taking apart and load carefully so nothing shifts on the road. Mirrors, pictures and glass are packed separately so they arrive in one piece.

Downsizing is common in this area. If you are leaving a family home for an apartment, we can help you plan what fits in the new place and move only what you are keeping.

Moving Near the Luas and UCD

The Green Line stops at Windy Arbour, Dundrum and Milltown make D14 popular with renters, and UCD's Belfield campus sits just across the Clonskeagh border. That means lots of smaller apartment and house share moves. A few tips:

  • Check whether your apartment block requires a booked lift or loading bay.
  • Avoid the busiest shopping hours around Dundrum Town Centre on Saturdays.
  • Book early for the start of the UCD academic year in September.
  • Have boxes sealed and labelled by room before the van arrives.

Roads and Access in D14

The Churchtown Road, Taney Road and Clonskeagh Road can all get busy at school times, and many estates have narrow roads with cars parked on both sides. Most houses do have driveways, which helps, but it is worth asking a neighbour to keep the road clear outside your door if you can. For larger homes we plan the load and number of trips in advance, or arrange a larger vehicle where that makes the day simpler.

Some homes near the Dodder at Clonskeagh and Milltown sit on sloping sites or have steps to the door. Let us know when booking and we will plan the carry accordingly.

If you are moving within D14, for example from a Goatstown semi to an apartment near Windy Arbour, we can often finish the job in a single morning, leaving you the afternoon to settle in.
