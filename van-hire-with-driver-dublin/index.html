Van Hire with Driver Dublin

Need a van and a driver without the hassle of hiring and driving it yourself? Moving Service Dublin provides van hire with an experienced driver across Dublin, fixed price, fully insured, and no need for a van license or unfamiliar city driving. It's one of several moving services we run across the city. It's a handy option for furniture removals, and if you're moving out of a flat, our apartment moving service takes care of the lot.

Why Hire With a Driver

No insurance headaches, no learning a new vehicle, and no navigating loading zones yourself. We drive, you focus on the move itself.

Flexible By the Job or by the Hour

Whether you need a single run or a few hours of van and driver time for multiple stops, we quote a fixed price based on what you actually need.

Understanding Van Hire with a Driver

Self drive van hire sounds cheap until you factor in insurance excess, an unfamiliar vehicle, and navigating Dublin traffic and parking yourself. Van hire with a driver gives you the vehicle and the labour without the risk of driving something you're not used to through the city.

Van Hire with Driver in Dublin: What to Expect

No License Requirements or Insurance Excess

You don't need a van license or to worry about hire company insurance excess, since our driver and vehicle come as one fixed price package.

Flexible for Multiple Stops

Whether it's one collection or several stops across Dublin, we can plan a route that covers everything in a single booking rather than multiple separate hires.

By the Job or by the Hour

Some jobs suit a single fixed price run, others work better booked by the hour if you need the van and driver on standby for multiple tasks during the day.

How to Choose Van Hire with a Driver

Questions to Ask

  • Is pricing fixed per job or clearly explained by the hour?
  • Is the driver experienced with Dublin traffic and loading restrictions?
  • Can the booking cover multiple stops in one trip?
  • Is the vehicle and load fully insured?

Tips for Booking Van Hire with a Driver

  1. Plan out all stops in advance if you need more than one drop off.
  2. Decide upfront whether a fixed job price or hourly booking suits you better.
  3. Have items ready to load at each stop to keep the day efficient.
  4. Confirm parking or loading access at each address.
