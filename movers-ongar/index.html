Movers Ongar Apartment and Duplex Owners Choose

Ongar was built as a planned village, with apartments above and around the shops on Main Street and estates of houses and duplexes spreading out towards Hansfield and Barnhill. That layout is great for living, but it means many moves start or finish in a building with shared access.

  • Apartment blocks: we confirm lift access, any booking system, and where the van can stop near the entrance.
  • Duplexes: own door upper units often have a steep internal stair, so we plan how bigger furniture will come down.
  • Parking courts: many homes share parking areas, so we keep the van tidy and out of neighbours' way.

Planning Around the Village Centre

The streets around Ongar's village centre can be busy, with on street parking, school traffic and deliveries to the shops. Where possible we load from the quieter side of a building or from a designated loading spot. If your management company has a preferred time for moves, tell us and we will fit around it. It also helps to have keys and fobs ready, and to know which doors lead most directly to the car park.

Moving From Your First Home

Ongar is home to a lot of first time buyers, and many of them eventually move on to a bigger house in Dublin 15 or further out in Meath and Kildare. Moving from a two bedroom apartment to a family home usually means more furniture arriving later, so do not worry if the van looks half full. It is also a good time to sort out what you no longer need. Bulky items you are getting rid of are best dealt with before moving day, as we move your belongings but do not offer house clearance.

Good Links for Moves Further Afield

Ongar sits between the N3 and the Ongar Distributor Road, with Hansfield station on the Dunboyne rail line nearby. For moves across Dublin, the N3 leads quickly to the M50. For moves into Meath and on to the North West, the M3 is right beside you at Clonee. We cover long distance removals anywhere in Ireland, so whether you are going to Navan, Cavan or Sligo, we can plan it.

We run one transit van with a driver and mover. All furniture is wrapped and protected, strapped securely and carried into the rooms you want at your new home. For larger homes we plan the load and trips in advance, or arrange a larger vehicle when needed. Smaller jobs are welcome, from a single wardrobe to a student move, and same day moves are possible when the van is free.

If you are moving out of a rental in Ongar, check your key return time and tell us when you ask for a quote. We will plan the day so the apartment is empty in good time for your final inspection.
