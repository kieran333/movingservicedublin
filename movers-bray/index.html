Movers Bray Homeowners and Renters Use

Bray has a wide mix of housing, which makes every move a little different. Near the seafront along Strand Road and around Quinsboro Road there are tall Victorian terraces and period houses split into flats, often with steep steps up to the front door and narrow internal stairs. Further back, areas like Ballywaltrim, Old Connaught and Woodbrook have family estates with driveways, and there are apartment blocks around the town centre and near the DART.

For period properties we plan carefully: we check the stairs, measure the tight spots, and dismantle larger pieces when needed. For flats in converted houses, we ask about shared hallways and which floor you are on so we can plan the carry.

Parking on the Seafront and Main Street

Parking is the main challenge in central Bray. Main Street is busy, and the seafront roads fill up quickly in good weather, especially on summer weekends. We look at the best place to stop in advance, and we often suggest weekday or early morning moves for properties close to the promenade. If you can keep a space free outside, it makes a real difference to the day.

Moving Within Bray

Lots of our Bray work happens inside the town itself, moving from a flat near the seafront to a house in Ballywaltrim, or from a family home in Woodbrook to an apartment closer to the shops and the DART. Short moves like these can often be done in a morning. Bray Head and the steep roads around Putland Road and Meath Road need a little more thought, because the van has to park on a slope. We use wheel chocks and secure every item before the doors open, so nothing rolls or slides while we load.

Bray to Dublin and Beyond

Bray is well placed for moves into Dublin. The M11 joins the M50 just north of the town, and a move into the city centre usually takes under an hour, depending on traffic. Morning traffic heading north on the N11 can be heavy, so we plan the run accordingly. We also cover moves further into Wicklow, to Greystones, Wicklow town or Arklow, and long distance removals anywhere in Ireland.

We run a single transit van with a driver and mover. Furniture is wrapped and protected, strapped in securely and placed where you want it. For bigger homes we plan the load and trips in advance, or arrange a larger vehicle if the job needs it. Smaller jobs like a single item or a student move are welcome too, and same day moves are possible when the van is free.

If you are moving into a period house in Bray, measure tight spots like the hall, the turn of the stairs and bedroom doors before moving day. Older homes often have narrow returns, and knowing sizes in advance helps us plan which pieces need to come apart.
